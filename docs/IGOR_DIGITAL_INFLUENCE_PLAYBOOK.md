# Igor Vepretski Digital Influence Playbook for 7ya.io

## Purpose

Make 7ya.io communicate Igor Vepretski's digital influence clearly, repeatedly, and verifiably without inflating claims that are not backed by evidence. The site should become the canonical public hub for Igor's story, proof, campaigns, media assets, and measurable calls to action.

## Core Positioning

Primary message:

> Igor Vepretski builds digital influence by turning personal story, youth responsibility, and civic technology into public infrastructure through #7YA, StartOn, and 7ya.io.

Supporting pillars:

1. **Identity** — present Igor / Ido / #7YA as one coherent public identity.
2. **Mission** — connect youth, civic responsibility, digital safety, and opportunity.
3. **Proof** — show source-backed work, public links, media, posts, projects, and archive records.
4. **Momentum** — make every page point to the next action: read, share, join, contact, or follow.
5. **Measurement** — use UTM discipline and analytics coverage so influence growth is visible instead of guessed.

## Homepage Changes

The homepage should immediately answer three questions:

- Who is Igor Vepretski?
- Why does his digital influence matter?
- What should visitors do next?

Recommended hero copy:

```text
Igor Vepretski turns digital attention into youth opportunity.

Through #7YA, StartOn, and 7ya.io, Igor connects personal story, civic responsibility, and practical technology into a public platform for young people, families, and communities.
```

Recommended primary calls to action:

- `Explore the #7YA story`
- `See the influence archive`
- `Partner with Igor`

## Required Site Sections

### 1. Influence Snapshot

Create a compact section near the top of the homepage with verifiable influence signals:

- public projects
- media references
- social channels
- campaign pages
- youth/community initiatives
- press kit link
- latest verified update date

Avoid vanity metrics unless they can be sourced and dated.

### 2. Story Hub

Create or promote a `/story/` route that explains Igor's journey in a bilingual, human, and searchable format. It should connect:

- Igor Vepretski
- Ido Vepretski
- #7YA
- StartOn
- youth opportunity
- digital sovereignty
- civic technology

### 3. Influence Archive

Create or promote a `/proof/` or `/archive/` route where each claim has a source, date, link, screenshot reference, or evidence ID. This route should include:

- official 7ya.io pages
- press kit
- public social posts
- media images
- project artifacts
- partner references
- campaign launches

### 4. Press and Partner Kit

Create or promote a `/press/` route containing:

- short bio
- long bio
- mission statement
- approved photos
- approved descriptions of #7YA and StartOn
- contact path
- schema metadata

### 5. Conversion Spine

Every public page should route visitors toward one of these actions:

- learn Igor's story
- share a canonical page
- contact / partner
- subscribe or follow
- visit `/pass/`
- visit `/radar/`

## SEO and Metadata Targets

Every relevant page should consistently include:

- `Igor Vepretski`
- `Ido Vepretski`
- `#7YA`
- `7ya.io`
- `StartOn`
- `digital influence`
- `youth opportunity`
- `civic technology`
- Hebrew equivalents where appropriate

Recommended homepage title:

```text
Igor Vepretski / #7YA — Digital Influence for Youth Opportunity
```

Recommended homepage description:

```text
7ya.io is the canonical public hub for Igor Vepretski, #7YA, StartOn, and a source-backed digital influence system for youth opportunity, civic responsibility, and practical technology.
```

## Content Engine

Use a repeatable weekly cycle:

1. Publish one canonical 7ya.io page or update.
2. Generate bilingual social posts from that page.
3. Add UTM-tagged links back to the canonical page.
4. Archive source evidence and screenshots.
5. Record analytics, engagement, and route checks.
6. Update the influence archive with verified proof.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim changes are live until GitHub Pages deployment succeeds and the live site is independently verified.
- Do not publish unsourced claims about influence, followers, partnerships, or impact.
- Do not store secrets, raw passwords, tokens, or private credentials in site content or ordinary documents.
- Keep 7ya.io as the canonical public result and GitHub as the code/deployment source of truth.
- Keep Datasite as the governance and evidence room.

## Minimum Implementation Pull Request

A first implementation PR in `7guard-io/7ya.io` should include only the minimum high-impact changes:

1. Homepage hero copy focused on Igor's digital influence.
2. New influence snapshot section.
3. Clear links to story, archive/proof, press, `/pass/`, and `/radar/`.
4. Updated title, description, Open Graph, and person schema metadata.
5. Route validation for homepage, assets, `/pass/`, `/radar/`, and required public pages.
6. Before/after screenshots for desktop and mobile.

## Success Criteria

7ya.io shows Igor Vepretski's digital influence “a lot” when:

- the homepage names Igor and #7YA above the fold;
- influence claims are backed by archive evidence;
- search metadata clearly associates Igor with 7ya.io, StartOn, and #7YA;
- visitors can move from story to proof to partnership in one or two clicks;
- social posts consistently point back to canonical 7ya.io pages;
- analytics can show which channels create meaningful traffic and engagement.
